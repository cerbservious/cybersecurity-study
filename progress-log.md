# Progress Log

One entry per study day. Short is fine — consistency beats length. This log is
your proof of work: future employers would rather see 60 days of steady
entries than a certificate with no trail behind it.

- **CC study:** domain / topic studied (from `cc-study/checklist.md`)
- **Key terms:** new vocabulary, in your own words
- **Stuck on:** anything confusing — revisit it in a few days
- **Practice scores:** (once you start taking them)
